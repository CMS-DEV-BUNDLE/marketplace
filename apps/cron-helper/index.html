Backend Gotchas & Best Practices
  • Day-of-Month vs Day-of-Week OR logic: In standard Linux crond, if both Day of Month and Day of Week are explicitly specified (neither is *), the job runs if either matches (logical OR, not AND).
  • UTC vs Local Clock: Cloud infrastructure (AWS EventBridge, GCP Cloud Scheduler, Kubernetes CronJobs) evaluates cron expressions in UTC by default unless explicitly configured with a timezone flag. Always verify if your job targets local or UTC time.
  • Sunday 0 vs 7: Standard cron considers 0 as Sunday. Some cron implementations (like BSD or Vixie cron) also accept 7 as Sunday. For maximum portability, prefer 0 or SUN.
  • Step syntax */N: */10 in the minute field means every 10 minutes starting at 0 (0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50). It does not mean "every 10 minutes from whenever the job was submitted".
